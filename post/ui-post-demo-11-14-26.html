ui-post：外框與 theme 設定

外框預設改為 gray 2px，邊框清楚可見。theme 可以一次設定外框與分隔線顏色，個別屬性（border-color、divider-color）會覆蓋它。

範例一：預設外框（gray 2px）

不寫任何外框屬性時，使用新的預設值。

How often do you exercise? I exercise almost every day. I go jogging every day. Name the sport instead of repeating "exercise". 
<ui-post author="IELTS Examiner" badge="Part 1" time="2h">
  ...留言內容相同...
</ui-post>

範例二：theme="violet"

theme 一次設定外框與分隔線的顏色。

How often do you exercise? I exercise almost every day. I go jogging every day. Name the sport instead of repeating "exercise". 
<ui-post theme="violet" author="IELTS Examiner" badge="Part 1" time="2h">
  ...

範例三：個別屬性覆蓋 theme

border-color、border-width 與 divider-color 可以各自指定，覆蓋 theme 的設定。

How often do you exercise? I exercise almost every day. I go jogging every day. Name the sport instead of repeating "exercise". 
<ui-post theme="violet" border-color="key" border-width="4px" divider-color="gray"
         author="IELTS Examiner" badge="Part 1" time="2h">
  ...

範例四：點擊板主頭像，跳出小視窗

設定 profile-target 後，點作者頭像會在旁邊跳出視窗，顯示 template 內的 HTML。再點一次、按 Esc、點視窗外或按 ✕ 都會關閉。

How often do you exercise? I go jogging every day. 
<ui-post profile-target="profile-nelson" profile-width="340px"
         author="Nelson IELTS" time="1h" badge="板主">
  ...
</ui-post>
