ui-stream：font-size 套用到 dashed 氣泡

修正前，style="dashed" 的氣泡會寫死 0.9rem，忽略元件的 font-size。修正後，氣泡繼承元件字級。

範例一：font-size="0.9rem"

candidate（dashed）與 examiner（filled）字級一致，皆為 0.9rem。

Do you think public spaces in your city are well maintained? Generally speaking, yes. A number of parks have been renovated in the past few years.
整體來說是的。過去幾年有許多公園完成翻新。 
<ui-stream autoplay title="應用示範：a number of" font-size="0.9rem" ...>
  <stream-role name="examiner" label="考官" side="left" style="filled"></stream-role>
  <stream-role name="candidate" label="高分考生" side="right" style="dashed"></stream-role>
  <stream-item from="examiner" typed>Do you think public spaces in your city are well maintained?</stream-item>
  <stream-item from="candidate" name="高分考生" typed><mark>A number of</mark> parks have been renovated.<br>整體來說是的。</stream-item>
</ui-stream>

範例二：font-size="1.4rem"

修正後 candidate（dashed）跟著變大；修正前會停在 0.9rem。

Do you think public spaces in your city are well maintained? Generally speaking, yes. A number of parks have been renovated in the past few years.
整體來說是的。過去幾年有許多公園完成翻新。 
<ui-stream autoplay title="應用示範：a number of（字級 1.4rem）" font-size="1.4rem" ...>
  ...同範例一的角色與訊息...
</ui-stream>

修改內容

flow/ui-stream.js 中 .us-dashed .us-bubble 的 font-size 由 0.9rem 改為 inherit。

/* 修改前 */
ui-stream .us-dashed .us-bubble{border-style:dashed;color:var(--us-rt,...);font-size:0.9rem;text-align:center;}

/* 修改後 */
ui-stream .us-dashed .us-bubble{border-style:dashed;color:var(--us-rt,...);font-size:inherit;text-align:center;}
