<ui-mask> 行內文字遮罩

點擊遮罩揭曉原文。可放在一般段落、ui-table 的格子、ui-cards 的卡片與面板裡。

六種遮罩樣式

  • solid：I wake up early to avoid the traffic.
  • stripe：It's a great place to unwind after work.
  • dots：I was so glad to find an excuse to skip the gym.
  • blur：Paper is likely to become less common.
  • underline：The library I visit most often is close to my place.
  • gradient：My parents were anxious to know my exam results.
I wake up early <ui-mask color="pink">to avoid the traffic</ui-mask>.
<ui-mask mask-style="stripe" color="yellow">...</ui-mask>
<ui-mask mask-style="blur" color="quiet" blur="6">...</ui-mask>
<ui-mask mask-style="gradient" gradient="4">...</ui-mask>

透明度 opacity

0 到 1，數字越小，原文透出越多，適合做「隱約可見」的提示。

  • 1：just a short bike ride from my place
  • 0.85：just a short bike ride from my place
  • 0.6：just a short bike ride from my place
  • 0.3：just a short bike ride from my place

區塊模式與提示文字

Unlike those who tend to have the same thing for lunch every day, I usually try to get something different, which is why I find food delivery apps really convenient. 
<ui-mask display="block" color="bg-alternative" label="先自己想想，再點我看答案" padding="14 18" radius="10">...</ui-mask>

v1.1.0：提示文字修正（11-11-26）

  • 內容比提示文字短：遮罩自動撐寬，不再擠成直排。go to bed early.
  • 行內遮罩直接顯示提示文字（label-mode="show"）：I usually take the bus to work.
  • 行內遮罩預設仍只當滑鼠提示：I usually take the bus to work.
  • 半透明遮罩加提示文字時，原文完全隱藏，不再和提示文字重疊：
Paper is likely to become less common in the future. 表格窄格裡的長提示文字：超過格寬時以 ... 截斷，不再被格子裁掉一半。x 
修改前（v1.0.0）：
<ui-mask display="inline-block" label="先想想再點">go</ui-mask>   提示文字被擠成一字一行，溢出蓋到上下行
<ui-mask label="點我看答案">take the bus</ui-mask>               行內模式只能當滑鼠提示，手機看不到

修改後（v1.1.0）：
<ui-mask display="inline-block" label="先想想再點">go</ui-mask>   遮罩撐到提示文字寬度，空間不足時以 ... 截斷
<ui-mask label="點我看答案" label-mode="show">take the bus</ui-mask>   行內也能直接顯示提示文字
<ui-mask display="block" opacity="0.5" label="先自己想想">...</ui-mask>   有提示文字時原文完全隱藏

滑過揭曉、再點一次遮回

  • trigger="hover"：滑鼠停留時暫時揭曉，移開就遮回。I'm planning to study abroad next year.
  • toggle：揭曉後再點一次可遮回。My goal is to work in Canada.

放在 ui-table 裡（含樹狀巢狀與可展開格子）

點遮罩只會揭曉，不會順便觸發格子的展開功能。

I wake up early to avoid the traffic. 目的 On exam days, I wake up an hour earlier than usual to review my notes one last time. 換你造句 After a long week, I was so glad to find an excuse to skip the gym and sleep in. This cell is expandable, so a long sentence is clamped to two lines until you click the text outside the mask. Clicking the mask only reveals it. 情緒的起因＋東西拿來做什麼

放在 ui-cards 裡（卡片與 template 面板）

點遮罩不會展開面板；點卡片其他地方才會展開。

It's hard to find a quiet café on weekends.
