chunk-demo 的 level-colors 支援品牌色名稱

修正前，level-colors 的每個顏色直接寫進樣式，填 green, yellow, violet 會顯示 CSS 原生的綠、黃、紫，不是品牌色；填 key 則完全失效。修正後每個顏色先轉換品牌色名稱，不分大小寫，theme 也不分大小寫。

修正後的實際元件

near my condo around the corner of my place just a five-minute walk from my rented apartment nice quite pleasant an absolute gem of a spot

程式碼對照

修正前

<chunk-demo theme="blue"
  level-colors="#71CD76, #E3D322, #B484E8"
  sentence="The cafe {0} is really {1}.">
  ...
</chunk-demo>

修正後（11-11-26）

<chunk-demo theme="Blue"
  level-colors="green, yellow, Violet"
  sentence="The cafe {0} is really {1}.">
  ...
</chunk-demo>
