ui-layer-diff v1.1：mode="comparison"

左側逐層比對，右側說明欄跟著層數切換。不需要另外寫 JavaScript。

基本用法：note 直接搬到右側

comparison 模式下，width 是左側方框的寬度，右側自動佔滿剩下的空間。

The library I visit most often The library I visit most often is conveniently located on a tree-lined street, The library I visit most often is conveniently located on a tree-lined street, just a short bike ride from my place. 
<ui-layer-diff mode="comparison" width="50%" theme="key" font-size="1.5" padding="20 28"
  legend="false" show-del="false" progress>
  <ui-layer label="主詞語塊" note="地點名詞加上關係子句，說明這是你最常去的地方。">The library I visit most often</ui-layer>
  <ui-layer label="位置語塊" note="...">...</ui-layer>
  <ui-layer label="距離語塊" note="...">...</ui-layer>
</ui-layer-diff>

自訂字體大小與色彩，用 note-tpl 放 HTML

note-tpl 填 <template> 的 id，右側顯示其 HTML，優先於 note。

The library I visit most often The library I visit most often is conveniently located on a tree-lined street, The library I visit most often is conveniently located on a tree-lined street, just a short bike ride from my place. 
<ui-layer-diff mode="comparison" width="55%" theme="violet" ...
  note-font-size="1.3" note-color="vanilla" note-bg="#161716" note-border="1px dashed violet"
  note-padding="20 24" note-gap="32" note-title-size="1.5" note-title-color="yellow">
  <ui-layer label="主詞語塊" note-tpl="lib-n1">...</ui-layer>
</ui-layer-diff>

<template id="lib-n1">
  <p>地點名詞加上關係子句，說明這是你最常去的地方。</p>
</template>

搭配自動播放，關閉右側標題，內容置頂

mode 可寫多個值，以空白分隔。

The café I go to most often The café I go to most often is tucked away in a quiet lane, The café I go to most often is tucked away in a quiet lane, just a five-minute walk from where I live. 
<ui-layer-diff mode="comparison auto" interval="3500" loop width="50%" theme="blue"
  note-title="false" note-align="top" note-font-size="1.2" note-color="blue"> ... </ui-layer-diff>

對照：原本的 manual 模式不受影響

沒寫 comparison 時，width 仍是整個元件的寬度，note 顯示在句子下方。

v1.2：載入時先不顯示說明

note-hide-initial 讓右側一開始保持空白，第一次切換層之後才出現說明；note-placeholder 可在空白期間放提示文字。開啟 progress 時，進度條在隱藏期間也保持全空。

The library I visit most often The library I visit most often is conveniently located on a tree-lined street, The library I visit most often is conveniently located on a tree-lined street, just a short bike ride from my place.

v1.1 寫法（一開始就顯示第一層說明）

<ui-layer-diff mode="comparison" width="50%" theme="key" ...>
  <ui-layer label="主詞語塊" note="...">...</ui-layer>
</ui-layer-diff>

v1.2 寫法（先隱藏，切換後才顯示）

<ui-layer-diff mode="comparison" width="50%" theme="key" ...
  note-hide-initial note-placeholder="先自己想想：這句話可以拆成哪幾塊？">
  <ui-layer label="主詞語塊" note="...">...</ui-layer>
</ui-layer-diff>
